This website and its booking form are not for emergencies. In a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

Prescription medicines

Prescription products shown in the catalog are dispensed only after consultation. Do not start, stop or share prescription skin medicines without the doctor's advice. This website does not promote any prescription drug to the public.

Photo & testimonial consent policy

  • Photographs of patients (including before/after images) are published only with the patient's specific, written consent for website and social-media use.
  • Consent is voluntary, is not a condition of treatment, and can be withdrawn at any time — withdrawn images will be removed.
  • Where a patient prefers, identifying features are cropped or obscured.
  • Patient stories and testimonials are published only with consent, and reviews quoted from public platforms are attributed to their source.

If you are a patient and would like an image or story removed, contact the clinic via the contact page and it will be taken down promptly.
