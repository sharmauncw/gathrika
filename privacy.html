Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains how Gathrika Skin Clinic ("the clinic", "we") handles personal data, consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

What we collect and why

When you request an appointment or send an enquiry, we collect only what is needed to arrange your care:

  • Your name, mobile number, age and gender — to book and confirm your appointment.
  • The service requested, preferred date and time, and a brief note of your concern — so the doctor can plan your visit.
  • Whether you are a new or returning patient.

We do not ask you to upload medical reports, photographs or detailed health history through this website. Please bring reports to your visit instead.

Consent

Booking details are collected only after you tick the consent boxes on the booking form. Consent for appointment-related messages on WhatsApp or SMS is taken separately. You may withdraw consent at any time by informing the clinic; withdrawal does not affect care already provided.

How your data is used

  • To confirm, remind you about, reschedule or cancel your appointment.
  • To maintain your clinic record when you become a patient.
  • Never for sale to third parties, and never for unrelated marketing.

Storage and security

In this first version of the website, booking requests made from your device are stored in your own browser (localStorage) and shown to you for managing your appointment; the clinic confirms your slot directly with you. As online systems are added, data will be stored in access-controlled systems in India, with access limited to the doctor and authorised clinic staff.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, and to ask for its erasure, subject to medical-record retention requirements under applicable law. To exercise these rights, visit the clinic or use the contact page.

Children's data

Appointments for children are booked by a parent or guardian, whose details are treated as the contact for the child's visit.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated and, where required, your consent sought first.

Grievances

For any privacy concern, please raise it with Dr. G. Kiranmayee at the clinic address given on the contact page. We will respond within a reasonable time.
