Meet your dermatologist

Dr. G. Kiranmayee completed her MBBS and her DDVL — a specialist diploma in Dermatology — at Gandhi Medical College, Hyderabad. She has been in medical practice for over 12 years and has specialised in skin, hair and nail care since 2017.

At Gathrika, every treatment plan is made by the doctor herself: careful diagnosis first, honest explanation in simple language, and only the treatment your skin actually needs.
